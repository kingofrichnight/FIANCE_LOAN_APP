Repayment score

Local history indicator only—not an official credit score, prediction or lending recommendation.

How this score works

Only installments with a non-zero amount due before today count. Each month has equal weight:

  • Fully paid by its due date: 100 points.
  • Fully paid late: 50 points.
  • Overdue and partially paid: 50 × received ÷ scheduled amount, rounded to two decimals.
  • Overdue with nothing recorded: 0 points.

The score is the average of those points, rounded down to a whole number. No scored months means “No history,” not zero.

Formula local-v1. This loan record only; separate entries are not combined. No external credit data or personal/contact details are used. Each installment has equal weight regardless of size or currency. Missing receipts count as unpaid, and changing receipts or terms changes the score. The app keeps one received total and latest date per installment, not every partial payment date. Days late do not change the fixed late-payment points. Do not use this indicator alone to make lending decisions.
